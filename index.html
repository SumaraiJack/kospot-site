Questions

Quick FAQ.

Is KosPot really free?

Yes. At launch, every feature is unlocked with no subscription, no paywall and no card. An optional Pro tier may land later via Google Play Billing — for now everyone gets everything.

Which diets does the planner support?

Standard, Vegan, Vegetarian, Anti-inflammatory, Diabetic-friendly, Carnivore, Keto, Mediterranean, Gluten-free, Halaal and Kosher, plus a Custom prompt for anything else — allergies, religious rules, fasting windows.

How accurate are the Rand price estimates?

Each item has an estimated range based on common SA retailers, refreshed weekly from the Shoprite, Checkers and Pick n Pay specials for your own province. The optional “paid R__” capture lets you log what you actually paid, so KosPot learns your real local prices.

Can I plan on a small budget?

Yes. Set any budget from a few hundred Rand and any number of days from 1 to 14. The plan and shopping list scale to fit.

What’s Share-to-KosPot?

Tap “Share” on a recipe link in Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or your browser and pick KosPot from the share sheet. We read the recipe and add the ingredients to your library.

Can I scan a recipe from a photo?

Yes. Paste a link from TikTok, Instagram or any blog, or photograph a printed or handwritten page — KosPot pulls out the ingredients and the method and saves it as a clean card.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete account. Everything tied to you is wiped within 7 days, per POPIA. Full steps are on the delete account page.

Where can I get the app?

Android via Google Play, launching soon. iOS is on the roadmap.
