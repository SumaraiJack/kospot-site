Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

KosPot ("we", "us", "the app") respects your privacy and complies with the South African Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and Google Play's Data Safety requirements. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how to control it.

KosPot was previously called ChowSA. It is the same app, the same developer and the same account — only the name changed. Nothing about how your data is handled changed with the rename.

The short version. Your pantry, your shopping lists and the photos you take of product labels never leave your phone. Your account, saved recipes and anything you post to the community are stored on our servers in the EU. Photos you scan are sent to Google's AI to be read, and are not kept by us afterwards. We do not sell anything about you.

What stays on your phone

Some of the app's most personal data is never uploaded anywhere. It lives in the app's own storage on your device, and it is gone the moment you uninstall.

  • Your pantry — every item, quantity, category and expiry date you add.
  • Your shopping lists — including anything shared into them from a recipe.
  • Product label photos — the two pictures taken when you add an item with its expiry date. These are stored on the device only, and are automatically deleted once the item passes its expiry date or you remove it from your pantry.
  • Expiry reminders — scheduled with Android's own alarm system. We are not told when one fires.
  • Your settings — theme, font, serving size, budget, and which tips you have dismissed.

Camera, microphone and location

Camera

Used only when you tap a scan button. Two things happen with a photo:

  • Fridge and cupboard scans are sent to Google Gemini to read the ingredients, and are discarded afterwards. We do not keep them.
  • Product and expiry-date photos are sent to Google Gemini to read the label, and a downscaled copy is kept on your device so you can check what we read. It deletes itself on the expiry date.

The app never opens the camera on its own and never takes a photo you did not ask for.

Microphone

Used only while you are dictating to Aura, the in-app assistant, and only after you tap the microphone button. Speech is turned into text by your device's own speech recogniser (Android's, or an on-device model you can optionally download). We do not upload, record or store your audio. The resulting text is treated like anything else you type to Aura.

Location

Approximate location, read only while the app is open, and only if you grant it. Used to find your nearest community hub and your load-shedding area. We store the resulting suburb or city, not a coordinate trail. There is no background location tracking of any kind.

Artificial intelligence

KosPot uses Google Gemini to generate recipes, read scanned photos, plan meals and power Aura. What gets sent depends on what you asked for: an ingredient list, a recipe name, a photo you scanned, or your message to Aura together with the app context needed to answer it (for example, what is in your pantry when you ask what you can cook).

  • We do not send your email address, your name or your account identifier with these requests.
  • Requests are relayed through our own server so that our API key never ships inside the app.
  • Recipes and answers generated for you are yours. We do not use your content to train any model.

Ads

The free tier shows occasional ads through Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads you can watch to unlock extra AI generations. AdMob uses your device's Advertising ID. Ads are served as non-personalised where required, and in regions where consent is legally required you are asked first. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android settings at any time.

What we do not collect

  • Precise background location. Location is read only with the app open.
  • Your contacts, SMS, call logs or browsing history.
  • Audio recordings. The microphone is used for live dictation only, and the audio is not sent to us.
  • Payment card details. KosPot is free. If an optional Pro tier ships later it will be billed entirely by Google Play Billing, and we would receive only an entitlement status — never card details.

Who we share with

  • Supabase — our database and authentication, hosted in the EU (Ireland). Their policy.
  • Google Gemini — AI recipe generation, photo reading and Aura. Their policy.
  • Google AdMob — ads in the free tier. Their policy.
  • Firebase (Google) — push notifications and crash reporting. Their policy. Not active in the current release; it will be enabled in a future update, and this page will say so before it is.

We never sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone not listed above.

Your rights under POPIA

  • Access — request a copy of everything we hold about you: chowsa.app.support@gmail.com.
  • Correction — edit your profile in the app, or email us.
  • Deletion — Settings → Privacy & Data Settings → Delete Account, or use our account deletion page. This is permanent and completes within 7 days.
  • Object — turn off notifications in Settings, or revoke camera, microphone or location permission in your Android settings. The app keeps working without any of them; the features that need them simply say so.
  • Complain — to the South African Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

Children

KosPot is intended for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us their information, email us and we will remove it.

Security

Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. Data at rest is encrypted by our hosting provider. Row-Level Security rules mean your rows can only be read by you and by anyone you have explicitly shared with — not by other users, and not by the app itself acting on your behalf.

Data retention

  • Active accounts — kept while your account is active.
  • Deleted accounts — permanently erased within 7 days of your request.
  • On-device data — pantry, lists and label photos are removed when you uninstall, and label photos delete themselves at their expiry date without you doing anything.
  • Moderation records — reports and blocks are kept for banned accounts to stop re-registration and protect other users.
  • Anonymous, aggregated statistics — for example how many people use a feature — are kept indefinitely, because they no longer identify anyone.

Changes

If anything changes materially we will update this page and say so in the app. Continuing to use KosPot after a change means you accept the updated policy.
